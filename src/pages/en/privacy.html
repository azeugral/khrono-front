Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026. Draft for presentation; reviewed by counsel before launch.

What we collect

Your account data (name, e-mail, phone), your business data (services, team, calendar, finances) and the clients you register to run the system. We also keep technical access logs.

What we use it for

  • Running the service: booking, confirming, charging, reporting.
  • Support and product improvement.
  • Messages about your account.

Who we share it with

Providers that run parts of the service on our behalf — hosting, messaging, payment processing — under contract and only as needed. We never sell data.

Your clients

For your clients' data, you are the controller and {{marca}} is the processor. We give you the tools to handle access, correction and deletion requests.

Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access and daily backups.

Your rights

Access, correction, portability and deletion, under the LGPD. Write to privacy@{{dominio}}.
