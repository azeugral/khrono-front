Questions

What people ask before starting

Do I need a card to try it?
No. Create the account, use it for seven days and only add a payment method if you want to carry on.
What is the difference between Basic and Pro?
Basic covers 1 business and includes the service catalogue, client management, team management, calendar and public booking. Pro covers up to 3 businesses and adds finances and payments, team permissions and the loyalty programme.
How does the yearly plan work?
You pay ten months up front and use twelve: R$ 599 on Basic (R$ 49.92 a month) or R$ 799 on Pro (R$ 66.58). You can switch plans at any time.
Do my clients need an account?
No. They book through your business link — in the bio, on social media, on a QR code at the counter — picking a service, a professional and a time. The client area is optional, to follow bookings and, on Pro, loyalty.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. It runs in the browser and adapts to phone and desktop, with nothing to install. Cancel whenever you like, from the dashboard.
